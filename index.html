Our Philosophy & Purpose

At Lilan Sports Academy, martial arts is not merely about combat techniques — it is a transformative path of character formation, mental focus, and moral integrity (Wu De - 武德).

We empower youth and adults through authentic Chinese martial arts (Kung Fu, Wushu Taolu, and Sanda combat), counteracting sedentary lifestyles and digital addictions with healthy athletic discipline, self-confidence, and true sportsmanship.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

✦ ❓ ✦
Do I need prior martial arts experience or high fitness to join?

Not at all. We welcome complete beginners of all ages and fitness levels. Our foundational Kung Fu and Wushu curriculum introduces stance work, flexibility drills, and breathing techniques gradually in a safe, supportive environment.

What is the minimum age for children to begin training?

Children can begin as young as 4 to 5 years old in our Little Dragons program. At this age, training focuses on motor coordination, balance, spatial awareness, focus, following instructions, and basic self-discipline.

What should I wear for my first free trial class?

Wear comfortable athletic clothing (such as track pants/shorts and a clean t-shirt) and bring a water bottle. Once you officially enroll, you will receive information regarding the official academy training uniform (Gi/Kung Fu suit) and protective gear.

What is the difference between Kung Fu, Wushu, and Sanda?

Kung Fu refers to traditional Chinese martial arts emphasizing classical hand and animal forms, internal energy, and discipline. Wushu (Taolu) is the standardized, acrobatic sport featuring high jumps, spins, and traditional weapons. Sanda is the full-contact combat sparring format combining kickboxing, punches, and takedowns.

How do belt gradings and ranking advancements work?

We conduct structured periodic belt evaluations where students demonstrate required stances, hand forms, weapon techniques, and physical conditioning. Every advancement reflects verified personal growth and technical mastery.
